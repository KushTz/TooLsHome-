Privacy Policy

How TOOLsHOME.cc collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 2026

📋 Overview

TOOLsHOME.cc ("we," "our," or "this site") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our free online tools and how we use it.

All tools on TOOLsHOME.cc run entirely in your browser. We do not store, transmit, or have access to any data you enter into our calculators or converters.

📊 Information We Collect

We may automatically collect limited, non-personally identifiable information through analytics tools, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • General geographic location (country or city level only)
  • Device type and browser information
  • Referring website (how you found us)

This information is collected in aggregate form only and cannot be used to identify you personally.

🍪 Cookies

TOOLsHOME.cc may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to support site functionality and analytics. These include:

  • Analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand site usage
  • Advertising cookies (e.g., Google AdSense) to serve relevant ads

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features.

📢 Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party providers, including Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your browsing history and interests.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

🔗 Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

👶 Children's Privacy

TOOLsHOME.com is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

🔒 Data Security

Since all tool calculations are performed locally in your browser, no input data is ever transmitted to our servers. We do not store any personal data entered into our tools.

For data collected through analytics and advertising (aggregate, non-personal data), we rely on industry-standard security measures maintained by our third-party providers.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
