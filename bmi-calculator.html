📋 BMI Categories
Underweight: BMI less than 18.5 — May indicate nutritional deficiency
Normal weight: BMI 18.5–24.9 — Healthy range for most adults
Overweight: BMI 25–29.9 — Slightly above healthy range
Obese Class I: BMI 30–34.9 — Increased health risk
Obese Class II+: BMI 35+ — High health risk, consult a doctor
